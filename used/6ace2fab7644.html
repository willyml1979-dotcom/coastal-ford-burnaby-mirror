Carefully equipped with the 302A High Equipment Group, XLT Sport Appearance Package, and FX4 Off-Road Package, our impressive One Owner 2023 Ford F-150 XLT SuperCrew 4X4 makes a bold statement in Agate Black Metallic! Powered by a Twin-Turbocharged 2.7L EcoBoost V6 delivering 325 horsepower, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission, this capable Four-Wheel Drive truck is ready for work, weekend adventures, and everything in between, achieving approximately 10.2L/100km on the highway. Its rugged styling is complemented by 20-inch dark alloy wheels, running boards, and a convenient tailgate step. The FX4 Off-Road Package adds extra capability for tackling more challenging terrain, while the Trailer Tow Package and 2.7L EcoBoost Payload Package enhance its versatility. Inside, the comfortable XLT cabin features black Sport cloth seating, heated power front seats, a power-adjustable driver's seat, dual-zone automatic climate control, and a leather-wrapped steering wheel. The 302A High Equipment Group also adds convenient features such as remote start, remote tailgate release, LED reflector headlights, LED fog lamps, and a SecuriCode keyless-entry keypad. Stay connected with SYNC 4, a 12-inch touchscreen, enhanced voice recognition, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi hotspot capability. Additional highlights include a 360-degree camera, power-adjustable pedals, and Ford Co-Pilot360 Assist 2.0 with advanced driver-assistance technologies. Ford's commitment to confidence and convenience makes this F-150 a compelling choice for drivers who need capability without sacrificing comfort.
